# Concrete Calculator — Full Website

A free, dependency-free concrete estimator website. Open `index.html` in any browser — no build step, no dependencies, no signup.

## What it includes

- **Working calculator** for 5 shapes: slab, trench footing, post hole, wall, and round column — with 5% / 10% / 15% waste buffer.
- **Outputs:** cubic yards, cubic feet, cubic meters, order quantity with waste, bag counts (80 / 60 / 50 / 40 lb), and 10-yd³ truck loads.
- **Guides:** all 30 tool categories, step-by-step how it works, exact formulas, ASTM bag yields, residential slab thickness guide, ready-mix vs. bags, worked examples, and FAQ.
- **Formulas:** slab `L × W × (T ÷ 12) ÷ 27`, trench `(W ÷ 12) × (D ÷ 12) × L ÷ 27`, cylinder `π × (Dia ÷ 24)² × depth/height`, wall `L × H × (T ÷ 12) ÷ 27`, order `yards × (1 + waste)`.

## Worked examples

- 10 × 10 ft slab at 4 in: ~1.23 yd³ with 10% waste (56 × 80 lb bags).
- 16 × 30 ft slab at 4 in: 5.93 yd³ exact, 6.52 yd³ to order with 10% waste.

## Safety note

Material estimates only. For load-bearing foundations, retaining walls over 4 feet, or structural columns, consult a licensed structural engineer and local building codes.
